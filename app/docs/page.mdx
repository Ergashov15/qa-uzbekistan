# Dasturiy Ta'minotni Sinash (QA) Qo'llanmalari

Dasturiy ta'minot sifatini ta'minlash (Quality Assurance) sohasiga oid barcha fundamental nazariy bilimlar, hayotiy sikllar, sinov darajalari va metodologiyalar to'plami.

Ushbu o'quv dasturi xalqaro **TpointTech Software Testing Tutorial** hamda **ISTQB** standartlari asosida to'liq adabiy o'zbek tilida bayon etilgan.

## SDLC Modellari (Software Development Life Cycle Models)

Dasturiy ta'minotni ishlab chiqish va sinash strategiyasini belgilovchi asosiy SDLC modellari:

<Cards num={2}>
  <Cards.Card
    title="🔄 SDLC Modellari Markazi"
    href="/docs/sdlc-models"
  >
    SDLC modellarining umumiy xaritasi, taqqoslash va har bir modelning sinov jarayonidagi o'rni.
  </Cards.Card>
  <Cards.Card
    title="🌊 1. Sharshara Modeli (Waterfall Model)"
    href="/docs/sdlc-models/waterfall-model"
  >
    Klassik chiziqli ketma-ket model: 7 ta bosqich, texnik-iqtisodiy asoslash va qat'iy talablar.
  </Cards.Card>
  <Cards.Card
    title="🌀 2. Spiral Modeli (Spiral Model)"
    href="/docs/sdlc-models/spiral-model"
  >
    Xatarlarni tahlil qilish va iterativ yondashuv: Katta va kichik o'zgarishlar, MS Excel moduli misoli.
  </Cards.Card>
  <Cards.Card
    title="🧩 3. Gibrid Model (Hybrid Model)"
    href="/docs/sdlc-models/hybrid-model"
  >
    Spiral + Prototip hamda V-Model + Prototip kombinatsiyalari va ko'p bosqichli amaliy jarayon.
  </Cards.Card>
  <Cards.Card
    title="🎨 4. Prototip Modeli (Prototype Model)"
    href="/docs/sdlc-models/prototype-model"
  >
    Mijoz tasdig'i uchun dastlabki ishchi model: Statik va Dinamik prototiplar hamda prototip sinovi.
  </Cards.Card>
  <Cards.Card
    title="⚡ 5. V-Model (Verification & Validation)"
    href="/docs/sdlc-models/v-model"
  >
    Verifikatsiya va Validatsiyaning 5 bosqichli parallel uyg'unligi, CRS vs SRS tafovutlari.
  </Cards.Card>
</Cards>
